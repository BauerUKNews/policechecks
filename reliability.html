Percentages are delayed or cancelled departures in the board checked, not historical punctuality. Boards cover the next 60 or 90 minutes; the window and number shown are stated for each station. All times are UK time.

When does a station raise an alert?

Alerts compare this station with the same UK time (within one hour) on weekdays or weekends over the past 28 days. At least three earlier comparable days are needed. An alert requires at least three affected departures and a rise of 10 percentage points or more above usual on repeat readings spanning at least five minutes. Empty boards and missing checks are excluded from the average. The graph shows one reading per recorded quarter hour; longer ranges average these readings.
